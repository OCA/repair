1. Enable **Analytic Accounting** in Accounting settings.
2. On the desired project, go to **Settings** tab and enable
   **Spread Timesheet Cost on Repair**.
3. On a repair order, configure the **Analytic Distribution**.
4. Register timesheets on the repair order linked to the project above.
5. When the repair order is confirmed as **Done**, analytic lines will be
   created for both the parts used and the timesheet costs, distributed
   according to the configured analytic distribution.
6. On cancellation or deletion of the repair order, the generated analytic
   lines are automatically removed (timesheet entries themselves are preserved).
