To create a quotation out of a repair order, you need to:

1.  Go to *Repair \> Repair Orders* and open a repair order.
2.  In the parts list, untick **Invoiceable** on every part that should not
    be billed to the customer.
3.  Click **Create Quotation**. The quotation is created with one line per
    invoiceable part.

To carry later changes of the repair order over to its quotation:

1.  Add, remove or replace parts, change their quantities, or tick and
    untick **Invoiceable**.
2.  Open the linked quotation. The banner reports that the repair order and
    the quotation are no longer synchronized.
3.  Click **Synchronize Lines**. Lines are added, updated, or set to a zero
    quantity, so that the quotation matches the invoiceable parts of the
    repair order.
