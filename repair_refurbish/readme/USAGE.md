1.  Create a repair.
2.  Indicate a product to repair, check the box *to refurbish*.
    If the product has a proposed refurbished version, it will be proposed
    to be used.
3.  Add operations if needed.
4.  Complete the repair.

The initial product will be moved to the 'Refurbish' location, and the
refurbished product will be moved from a 'Refurbish' location to the
desired destination location.

In the traceability report, the refurbished product unfolds to the
initial product and to the parts added, removed or recycled during the
repair, like a manufacturing order does with its components. The initial
product keeps its own history, so the lot or serial number of the
refurbished product can be traced back to the full history of the
initial one. The refurbished product and its lot or serial number are
also shown in the printed repair order report.
