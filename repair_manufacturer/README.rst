===================
Repair Manufacturer
===================

This module extends Odoo's standard features to manage electronic device repairs by introducing manufacturer/brand tracking.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

To configure this module:

1. Navigate to **Contacts**.
2. Open a partner and check the **Is Manufacturer** field to designate them as a manufacturer.
3. Configure your repair or product settings as needed.

Usage
=====

To utilize this module:

1. Go to the **Repair** application module dashboard.
2. Create or open a **Repair Order**.
3. Associate manufacturers or brands to streamline your repair workflows.
